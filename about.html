Our Story

Where Shraddha Meets Craftsmanship

What began as one devotee hand-stitching vastras for her home mandir has grown into a platform serving individual devotees, temples, spiritual organisations and the global Indian community.

Every mukut, mala and poshak is handcrafted by our artisan collective — many of them rural women whose families have practised these crafts for generations. We pair their hands with technology: AI measurement, temple dashboards, and donation tracking — so seva becomes effortless without losing its soul.

🎯 Our Mission

To make devotional adornment more accessible, personalized, sustainable and meaningful for devotees and temples around the world.

🌏 Our Vision

To become the world's leading devotional adornment and temple services platform by combining tradition, craftsmanship, technology and sustainability.
